# Calibration and track records

A voice is accountable for its original position, not merely its persuasiveness. Before recommending, reread relevant histories by decision type; the Chairman explains in words when a past error changes present advice. Few noisy outcomes cannot justify numeric seat weights, rankings of expertise or confidence scores. All voices share one model, not independent evidence.

Compare a new contribution to its full card and the matching deeper reference actually read: mechanism, distinctive question, real objection addressed, limitation and condition of revision. Try a case in which its typical answer should lose. Insistence alone does not change a view; changed facts or priorities can. The owner may reject an interpretation without that rejection proving denial or resistance. Reasoned agreement is allowed; do not stage disagreement for show.

At follow-up ask what action was actually tried, what was reported or observed, dates, baseline and competing explanations. Success of the chosen path does not validate an untried alternative. A good process may lose; a poor one may get lucky. Long-range direction can have only early signals at 60 days. Missing or premature evidence needs a reason, not a verdict presented as certainty.

On the owner's yes append one entry per strong-position voice, including structural roles and the Chairman, using `write_page`, `author: counterpart`: date; sitting canonical path; decision type; original position; chosen action; baseline/prediction/failure condition; reported or observed result with source; **RIGHT / WRONG / PARTIAL / N/A** and why. N/A means untested if the advised action was not attempted; explicitly state missing/premature evidence where relevant. The paired seat's single history keeps the two positions distinct. Preserve prior positions and judgments; never rewrite an earlier prediction to fit the outcome.

Append actual outcomes and unresolved signals to the sitting and only then offer to close the check, following [council-landing.md](council-landing.md). A proposed check is a dated task, not autonomous monitoring.

Do not call voice fidelity certified or the full sitting → decision → task → check cycle proved until observed in this room. Record dated process lessons only when they happened and keeping them is authorized. A preparation receipt establishes which library files were read, not independent expertise. Identify a missing distinction and request relevant owner-supplied evidence rather than inventing authority.
