# Article delivery and handoffs

> Reference for writing-pack-substack-newsletter. Read when delivering, saving, revising, or handing off after approval.

The canonical output is one clean article. Conversation can hold options, analysis, proof gaps, and receipts, but the article itself should be ready for continued editing or pasting into Substack.

## The article

Write the full article only when there is a meaningful approved direction or the owner explicitly requests a fast draft. Title it with the selected working title. The body contains:

1. article title;
2. subtitle when approved;
3. complete article body;
4. an explicit paywall marker (`— PAYWALL —`) only when the owner approved one;
5. the article CTA when applicable.

Do not put editorial commentary, title alternatives, research receipts, hidden instructions, proof-gap reminders, or source-analysis notes inside the publishable body. Keep those in chat unless the owner asks for an editorial appendix.

## Where it lives

- Always: the full article in the chat.
- In the room: on their yes, a page in their library via `write_page` (`author: counterpart`), titled `Newsletter draft — <working title>` (an earlier draft may carry the newsletter's own name in place of «Newsletter», so find the earlier page by its working title, whatever comes before the dash). Revisions are dated `append`s carrying the full revised article, never a second page. When the owner approves or turns it down, append one line saying which and why.
- Terminal: the brain's notebooks, under `ideas-lab/substack-newsletter/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`, revised in place.

If the owner supplies an existing draft, confirm it is the intended target and revise that one. Preserve accepted sections when revising a local problem.

## Research receipts

After substantive article, creator, market, or research on the owner's record, cite only sources that were actually opened and state the contribution of each.

## Handoff after approval

Keeping an article is not permission to publish. After the article is approved, ask whether the owner wants to:

- keep it as a page;
- get a paste-ready version for the Substack editor (clean markdown, headings, the paywall marker where the owner will place Substack's paywall);
- put a task on the board to publish it on a given day (on their yes, with the working title in the task);
- turn it into Substack Notes.

Publishing, scheduling and setting the paywall in Substack are their own acts in their own Substack account. Do not silently rewrite approved copy during handoff.

## Substack Notes handoff

An article and a Notes portfolio are different editorial products. If the owner wants Notes, hand the approved article to `writing-pack-substack-notes`. Do not automatically reduce headings to short posts or copy article sentences into Notes. The Notes skill mines independent angles and produces its own balanced portfolio.

## Corrections and ownership

If a source is deleted, unavailable, or rejected, report the limitation and stop relying on it. Never claim an action succeeded without a receipt. Nothing is published, scheduled, or sent from here.
