# Editorial directions and structures

> Reference for writing-pack-substack-newsletter. Read when choosing a route or a structure.

Structure is a consequence of the editorial job. Do not force every newsletter into APAG, a listicle, or a personal story. First decide what kind of change the article must create, then choose the lightest architecture that can deliver it. Each worked reference named below is described in `reference-articles.md`; read the actual article before leaning on it.

## Educational or mechanism-led

Use when the reader needs to understand how something works and apply that understanding. A useful progression is problem or desired outcome, mechanism, implications, practical model, examples, and next action. The mechanism must explain more than the advice alone. Dan Koe's articulation article is a reference for moving from personal stakes to a central model and then through progressively deeper frameworks. Study the progression, not the language or persona.

## Personal or narrative

Use when the owner's owned experience is the proof or when a change in belief is best shown through a scene. Begin as close as possible to the meaningful tension. Move through choice, consequence, interpretation, and reader relevance. A personal opening must earn the broader point; autobiography without transformation is not an article.

## Argument or contrarian essay

Use when the owner disputes common advice, names a cultural tension, or needs to defend a position. State the real opposing belief fairly, explain why it persists, present the thesis, support it with evidence and examples, address the strongest counterargument, and show the practical consequence. Dan Koe's multiple-interests article is a reference for combining identity recognition with contrarian positioning. Do not inherit its worldview automatically.

## Conceptual diagnosis

Use when a memorable concept can compress a diffuse problem into something the reader can see. Define the concept, show its mechanism, map its consequences, distinguish it from nearby ideas, and offer a response. Gurwinder's intellectual-obesity article is a reference for carrying a coined concept and analogy through a full diagnosis. A coined phrase is only useful when it clarifies reality rather than decorating an ordinary claim.

## Transformation explainer

Use when a reader wants to change behavior and a research-backed mechanism can make the path credible. Start with the felt struggle, explain the mechanism in accessible language, connect it to the transformation, make the limits of the evidence clear, and translate it into realistic actions. The ixcarus neuroplasticity article is a reference for mechanism-to-application progression. Do not overstate science or turn correlation into certainty.

## Cross-domain synthesis

Use when their original advantage comes from connecting disciplines — their own trade and systems, craft and technology. Begin with one concrete problem, introduce only the outside concept that changes how the reader sees it, explain the correspondence faithfully, and return to an original practical consequence. The analogy must preserve the important differences between domains. More references do not automatically create deeper synthesis.

## Other useful architectures

- **Answer-first pyramid:** conclusion, supporting arguments, evidence, implications.
- **Problem-mechanism-solution:** recognizable problem, causal explanation, useful response.
- **APAG:** attention, problem, agitate, gain. Use for a clear practical promise, not by default.
- **Question-led investigation:** consequential question, competing explanations, evidence, conclusion, remaining uncertainty.
- **Field guide:** situation, diagnostic signals, principles, options, examples, decision rule.

## Direction test

Before outlining, answer:

1. What is the thesis in one sentence?
2. What should change for the reader?
3. What proof makes this credible?
4. Why is this writer entitled to make the argument?
5. Which section would be missing if the chosen structure were removed?

If the same generic outline fits every proposed route, the directions are cosmetic. Return to the reader tension and develop genuinely different arguments.
