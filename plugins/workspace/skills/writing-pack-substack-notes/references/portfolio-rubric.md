# Substack Notes portfolio and quality rubric

> Reference for writing-pack-substack-notes. Read when critiquing a Note or a batch, and for the final release check.

Judge a Notes batch at two levels: the quality of each individual Note and the editorial balance of the portfolio. A batch can contain six competent Notes and still fail because every Note makes the same claim in the same emotional register.

## Individual Note checks

### Independence

Can a reader understand and value the Note without seeing the source article, previous Note, or editorial explanation? A bridge may point outward, but it must still stand on its own.

### Ownership

Does the Note rely on the owner's actual experience, evidence, observation, worldview, or material on their record? Remove invented personal details, borrowed authority, and generic claims dressed as conviction.

### Specificity

Replace abstract nouns with mechanisms, examples, images, choices, or consequences. Specificity does not require a statistic; it requires something the reader can picture, test, or distinguish.

### Progression

Does each sentence or bullet change what the reader knows or feels? Remove throat-clearing, duplicated premises, and endings that merely repeat the opening.

### Voice fit

Does the language match their voice rules and their current intent? Preserve warmth, uncertainty, humor, directness, or reflection when they are supported. Do not turn them into a high-conviction internet copywriter.

### Originality

Can the Note be traced to their material rather than to a reference author's wording, image, or distinctive rhythm? Structural inspiration is acceptable; recognizable imitation is not.

## Portfolio role checks

- **Discovery x2:** clear to a stranger, relevant without prior context, distinct from each other.
- **Depth x1:** develops a mechanism, argument, example, or useful practice beyond a slogan.
- **Voice x1:** reveals taste, perspective, lived texture, or a memorable way of noticing.
- **Publication Bridge x1:** points to a real connected asset only after delivering standalone value.
- **Conversation x1:** invites a specific answer with room for disagreement or experience.

If no honest bridge exists, replace it. Never fabricate an article or repeat a link just to fill the taxonomy.

## Batch diversity checks

Compare angle, opening family, structure, length, tone, emotional register, ending, and CTA. At least four dimensions should vary across the portfolio. Remove two Notes that compete for the same job and replace the weaker one with an underserved role.

## Final release check

Before delivering, confirm that every publishable body is clean text without labels, citations, or editor notes. The editorial role sits in the label above the Note, never inside it. State any proof gap outside the Note. The owner should be able to copy one Note and post it without cleaning up the copy first.
