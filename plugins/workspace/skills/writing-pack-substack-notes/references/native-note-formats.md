# Substack-native Note formats

> Reference for writing-pack-substack-notes. Read when choosing the form of a Note.

Format should serve the idea. Substack Notes can hold more warmth, reflection, and conversational texture than a character-limited post, but length alone does not create depth. Choose the smallest form that completes the reader experience.

## Direct recommendation

Open with a clear recommendation, give a short set of concrete ways to act on it, and end with the practical or emotional payoff. This works when the advice is specific and the reason is earned. Avoid generic commands that could apply to anyone.

## Imperative, examples, mechanism

Begin with an energetic action, show several ordinary examples, then explain the mechanism that makes the action useful. The force comes from movement and specificity, not capital letters. Use sparingly when the writer genuinely has conviction.

## Emotional reframe

Name the painful interpretation a reader may be carrying, offer a more accurate frame, and show what changes under the new interpretation. Short fragments can create cadence, but they must clarify rather than manufacture drama.

## Poetic observation

Accumulate concrete details, images, or ordinary desires until they reveal a worldview. This format can build intimacy and voice without making a formal argument. Keep the details owned and observable. Do not copy another writer's imagery or cadence.

## Identity recognition list

Name a recognizable group or state and list specific behaviors that make the reader feel seen. The bullets should be independent and nonredundant. Avoid flattening people into stereotypes. A follow or subscription CTA is optional and should appear only when the owner explicitly wants a growth-oriented ending.

## Analogy plus insight

Use a familiar system to explain an unfamiliar mechanism, then state the implication. An analogy is useful only if the mapping holds. Name where it stops being exact when the boundary matters.

## Scene to implication

Begin with a small lived moment, conversation, mistake, or observation. Let the scene earn a broader insight. Do not invent autobiographical details, and do not polish away the detail that makes the scene credible.

## Practical mini-essay

State a focused claim, develop it through one mechanism or example, then end with a decision, boundary, or application. This is the best default for a Depth Note. It should still be narrower than a newsletter section.

## Conversation prompt

Supply enough context that the question is interesting before asking it. Good questions expose tradeoffs, invite examples, or request a bounded judgment. Avoid empty engagement prompts such as "Thoughts?" or questions whose only acceptable answer agrees with the author.

## Publication bridge

Deliver a complete insight from the connected article, explain why the deeper material exists, and link only when the article genuinely continues the reader's interest. One portfolio slot is enough. Notes should not feel like repeated distribution wrappers for the same link.
