# Finding note-worthy angles

> Reference for writing-pack-substack-notes. Read when mining angles from long writing or a reference Note.

A source becomes useful for Substack Notes when it contains more than a topic. The work is to find the claims, tensions, details, mechanisms, scenes, questions, and implications that can each support an independent reading experience.

## The angle test

An angle should answer four questions:

1. What is the one thing this Note is really about?
2. Why would the intended reader care today?
3. What makes this observation specific to the writer's evidence or perspective?
4. What should remain with the reader after the final line?

"Creativity" is a topic. "Changing rooms can interrupt repeated thought patterns" is an angle. "AI in music" is a topic. "The tools that save the most time are the ones that make the fewest creative decisions" is an angle.

## Sources of useful angles

- **Tension:** two things that appear compatible but are not, or a cost hidden inside a desirable behavior.
- **Mechanism:** why a familiar result happens, expressed in concrete cause and effect.
- **Observation:** a precise detail from work, life, a session, a conversation, or a repeated reader pattern.
- **Reframe:** a more useful interpretation of a frustrating experience or common belief.
- **Instruction:** one recommendation with enough reasoning and boundaries to be trusted.
- **Identity recognition:** a pattern that helps a reader recognize themselves without turning them into a stereotype.
- **Analogy:** a familiar system that makes an unfamiliar idea easier to understand.
- **Open question:** a genuine uncertainty with a constrained and interesting answer space.

## Extracting from long-form writing

Do not summarize the article six times. Look for separate units:

- the central argument;
- one surprising supporting mechanism;
- one story or image that can stand alone;
- one practical implication;
- one unresolved question;
- one bridge back to the full article.

The Publication Bridge should reveal enough value to stand alone before offering the article as a deeper next step. Avoid "I wrote about this, link below" as the entire Note.

## Extracting from reference Notes

Describe the reference in structural terms. Examples include imperative plus examples plus payoff, poetic accumulation plus shared longing, analogy plus expert implication, or identity list plus recognition. Then rebuild the mechanism using the owner's own source material. If the new Note would still feel recognizably like the reference author after removing names, the transformation is not original enough.

## Selecting the six

Score candidate angles for reader relevance, specificity, ownership, independence, novelty relative to the batch, and fit with an editorial role. Favor a varied set over six individually polished versions of one thesis. A strong portfolio should make the publication feel dimensional: useful enough to follow, distinctive enough to remember, and open enough to talk with.
