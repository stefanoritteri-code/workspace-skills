# Offer Blueprint contract

> Reference for writing-pack-offer-design. Read when delivering, saving or revising the blueprint.

ONE blueprint per offer, revised in place. The chat carries the diagnosis, the coaching and the questions; the blueprint carries only the blueprint.

## The deliverable

Title: `Offer Blueprint — <product>`, where `<product>` is the short product name. The body contains exactly these sections, as markdown headings, in this order:

1. **Target Audience** — the ideal customer in one tight paragraph, ending with the diagnosed awareness level (unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware, most-aware).
2. **Delivery Mechanism** — the format that maximizes perceived value, and why.
3. **Big Idea** — one concise, attention-grabbing articulation of the offer's promise.
4. **Big Problem** — the primary pain, framed for the audience's awareness level.
5. **Irking Pain Points** — a progressive bulleted list, each more specific and emotionally sharper than the last.
6. **Quantifiable Outcome** — a specific, measurable result with a timeframe.
7. **Risk Reversal** — the guarantee, with its conditions stated plainly.
8. **Unique Mechanism** — the named system, laid out in three to six real steps.
9. **Features & Benefits** — bulleted, each line "feature: the obstacle it removes or the outcome it advances."
10. **Value Equation Analysis** — four short entries (Dream Outcome, Perceived Likelihood, Time Delay, Effort & Sacrifice) naming what the offer does for each lever and where it is still weak.
11. **Positioning Recommendations** — how to communicate this offer across the owner's channels.

Mark every component built by inference rather than their verified facts with *(suggestion)* so the owner can see exactly what to confirm.

## Where it lives

- Always: the full blueprint in the chat.
- In the room: on the owner's yes, a page in their library via `write_page` (`author: counterpart`, title as above). Refinements are dated `append`s carrying the full revised blueprint, never a second page.
- Terminal: the brain's notebooks, under `ideas-lab/offer-architect/<product-slug>.md`, revised in place. Search for an existing blueprint by title before creating one.

## Revising

Every refinement delivers the FULL revised blueprint — never create a second blueprint for the same product. A different product gets its own blueprint.

## What stays out of the blueprint

No interview transcript, no meta-commentary, no praise, no "next steps" section. After delivering, ask in chat — outside the blueprint — which components to refine, and offer the `writing-pack-landing-page-copy` handoff once it is settled: the blueprint is exactly the input that skill reads first.
