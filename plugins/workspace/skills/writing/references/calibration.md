# Calibrate the owner's voice

Read [voice.md](voice.md) for retrieval, register boundaries and factual discipline. Calibration needs three or more texts actually written by the owner, pasted, uploaded or already imported and retrieved through the door. Read each relevant text fully, keeping its source, date, channel, audience and language. Incoming messages, generated drafts and another author's references do not count. Prefer the same register; if samples span registers, separate the observations and their scopes instead of blending them.

With fewer than three owned texts, explain what is missing and ask for the remaining samples. If the owner wants to write now, use the provisional draft route in voice.md; do not call that completed calibration or claim to have learned their voice from one text.

Present concrete observations tied to passages: sentence lengths and variation, rhythm, how they open and close, punctuation, vocabulary and degree of familiarity. Distinguish repeated evidence from a one-off choice. Words absent from these samples are only absent here; ask the owner which words they never use before treating an exclusion as a rule. Do not infer personality or a universal voice from one recipient or channel.

After naming the filed rules being followed as voice.md requires, write a short trial on the owner's actual subject in the observed voice. Label unaccepted observations as hypotheses, separate from filed rules. Ask what sounds right and what they would change; apply their correction to the trial.

For every observation the owner accepts as a future guide and every correction they ask to remember, obtain their own formulation and its scope. A yes to a proposed observation authorizes keeping it but does not turn our sentence into their words. Ask only for missing wording or scope; never broaden a local edit into a rule. File each accepted observation as a scoped rule using [landing.md](landing.md): a Voice page for source-linked evidence and exact owner words, then the live decision with the confirmed page path and at most one project-defined scope tag. Supersede an existing changed live rule by its actual ID, preserving its scope; standing changes use the operator route.

Read back each confirmed rule, its scope and filing receipt in plain language. A page alone is not a filed rule. Held or uncertain acts remain unconfirmed and block dependent writes; preserve partial results without duplicate retries. An accepted example can be kept with its verdict and the owner's reason; it does not replace the scoped rule. Calibration ends with the short corrected trial, confirmed rules and any remaining gaps. Later retrieval and application on a new text are a separate verification of learning, not a claim made by this session alone.
