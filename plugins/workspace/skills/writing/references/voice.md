# The owner's voice

Establish text type, recipient, purpose, language and intended substance from what is already given; ask only what changes the draft. Artifact language can differ from conversation language. Read standing/live topic rules, then separately the actual register's scope, such as `scope:writing` or `scope:work-email` only where adopted. General rules still apply, but do not transfer a post convention into work email. If a decisive rule read fails, offer a clearly provisional draft from current instructions.

Retrieve owned samples with `recall` and `read_source`, including accepted/rejected verdicts and reasons. Preserve author, date, channel, language, recipient and corrections. Unapproved drafts are not positive examples; incoming email teaches context, not the owner's voice. For voice calibration read [calibration.md](calibration.md) and use three or more owned texts. With fewer, request the missing samples; if immediate drafting is wanted, use the available material with an explicit provisional limit. With none, ask what they want to say in their words and produce a provisional draft. Never claim to have learned a voice from one text or label a provisional draft completed calibration.

Observe sentence rhythm and length, syntax, punctuation/spacing, concrete vocabulary and craft terms, familiarity, distance from the reader, explicitness, evidenced irony, opening, development, examples and stopping. Tie hypotheses to actual passages. Repetition is not a future obligation; formality may belong to a recipient rather than a general voice. No psychological traits are inferred.

Start from real raw material: a spoken phrase, note, previous text or verified fact. Preserve the owner's point and what already works; edit only as much as needed. If another writer supplies a structural reference, extract functions—example, question, objection, development, conclusion—without copying voice, phrases or biography. No universal structure or imposed literary personality.

Distinguish opinion from its factual basis. Read a source before answering it; verify decisive factual claims through supplied material, the door or an identified available source connector. Missing access becomes an explicit gap. Do not invent experiences, meetings, emotions, promises, people, quotations or numbers to strengthen the voice. Keep substantive placeholders out of a ready text.

Before writing any draft or revised variant, tell the owner briefly which filed rules you are following, naming their wording and scope from actual reads. If none were found, say so; if retrieval failed, say that instead of claiming there are no rules. Keep unfiled sample-based observations distinct from those rules.

Deliver a complete draft in the requested length/format. Variants only when they support a real choice. Put brief scope/uncertainty notes outside the owner's text. Compare line by line with live rules and same-register examples: facts, intent, voice, rhythm, opening/ending, exclusions and cross-channel leakage. Revise alien phrases before delivery. This is self-review, not independent certification, and it authorizes no send or publication.

Apply a correction immediately. Distinguish accepted, corrected, rejected and misunderstood brief; a wrong-brief draft does not teach a stylistic rule. When keeping is authorized, preserve before/after, exact owner judgment and scope. “Remove that joke here” changes this piece; “remember, no jokes in work email” supplies intent, wording and scope without another yes. Ask about a future rule only when necessary. A yes to our proposed rule still needs the owner's formulation.

Use [landing.md](landing.md): a Voice page holds text, comparison and judged examples; an adopted future rule goes into the live record with `page`, superseding a changed live rule with its actual ID and no unsupported tags/project fields. Do not silently update project instructions, skills or external originals. A chosen test on the next piece gets a task with an agreed deadline; no fabricated follow-up.

Prove learning by retrieval → a new relevant text → showing the applied correction → respecting an out-of-scope case → honoring later supersession. Saved, reread and verified are separate outcomes. If the record is missing in a new conversation, retrieve it; do not pretend memory. Review-only requests use [review.md](review.md) and return findings rather than an unrequested rewrite.
