# Publicity and campaign copy

Start with real work and its intended audience. Identify occasion, purpose, chosen channels, materials and actual capacity. Distinguish announcement, press approach, story of the work, biography and accompanying text. Read current tasks/waits/projects, relevant relationship notes only where needed, past campaign pages and same-register voice corrections via [voice.md](voice.md). If a draft was requested, write it without another ritual. A new opportunity remains a brief proposal with recipient, reason, timing and required material, not an adopted campaign.

Use what the owner actually said, thought or observed; ask only if that substance is absent. Invent no emotions, meetings, credits or quotations. A new voice is a proposed register to choose. From a supplied reference extract functional sequence, not distinctive sentences/cadence or biography. Visual references need actual visible images; an inaccessible title/link is insufficient.

Check recent facts, numbers and distribution claims against material actually supplied or an identified permitted source connector, retaining provenance/date. If no route exists, name the owner-browser → relevant excerpt/upload route. Quotes stay exact, summaries stay ours. Missing claims remain in the review package as gaps, not publishable placeholders.

Compare a few genuinely different campaign directions only when needed: who must understand what, proof, channel, moment and preparation cost. Each piece has a real function, responsible person and identified inputs. A pause or one strong piece can be valid. No default frequencies, hashtag bans or imported aesthetics. Before dates read relevant accepted days and available calendar evidence; unread time is not free.

Press/contact suggestions require a specific evidenced fit, actual contact route and known relationship history. A public role alone does not show interest; never fabricate contacts. Distinguish proposed invitation, actual send, reply and obtained coverage. Two interchangeable pitches probably lack a recipient-specific reason.

Deliver complete copy: title/subject, opening, body, ending, facts/source notes, associated image/file, credits, verified links and proposed date where relevant. Short/long versions only for chosen channels. Check every claim and voice choice against the brief; remove filler and unearned emphasis without substituting our personality. Flag missing specific announcement/material authorization instead of inferring it from file presence. For visuals name inputs and actual editor/production route; a board is not a video.

For selected correspondence follow [mail.md](mail.md). No sending, scheduling, tagging or automatic posts, even through a capable connector. The owner checks and publishes in their app. Only evidence or the owner's report establishes an actual send/publication, and only then may an adopted response wait start. Use [work-lifecycle.md](work-lifecycle.md) for actual state changes.

File with [landing.md](landing.md): Publicity campaign page, exact adopted decision with `page`, agreed preparation/result-review task and deadline. At review compare the original objective to relevant replies, enquiries, reads or actions by channel/period. Reach alone does not prove a successful campaign or better voice. Preserve exact corrections and their scope for the next text.
