# Briefing from existing material

Fix topic, audience, purpose, period and depth. If sharing scope is unclear, prepare the permitted internal version and clarify audience before including external-facing details. Do not distribute. Read the relevant record, pages and owner-supplied/designated documents; operational status comes from current tasks/waits and relevant people/journal, not an old plan. Do not fill a gap with unsolicited outside investigation.

For each source retain title/path, document date, period covered, part actually read, provenance and limitations. Decisive claims need traceable citations with page numbers where present or actual section locators. Quotes are verbatim and attributed; summaries are ours. Record conflicting versions and what would resolve them. Today's compilation date does not make an old source current.

Heading: “Briefing — <topic>”, preparation date, latest actually verified source date and decisive coverage gaps. Open with the situation, what changed, the central question and whether it is answered or open. Attribute positions and recommendations to their sources rather than slipping in our advice.

Include only sections the material supports: essential point; short history; what actually exists versus proposed capabilities; positions and live decisions; operational state and dates; necessary numbers with units/period/source; open questions and documented alternatives with attributed benefits/costs; adopted next steps and proposed checks; sources and coverage. Prose explains, tables compare. No “obvious choice” labels disguising advice or psychology.

For an update reread changed sources and append a new, dated complete synthesis to the canonical topic page, preserving earlier readings as history. The door does not overwrite pages. Follow [landing.md](landing.md) for any adopted decision and dated follow-up; a briefing without a new decision does not manufacture one.

If a laid-out document is requested, name the real document editor/export/renderer operation or the human local build route. A verified PDF needs actual export and inspection of rendered pages for clipped tables, orphaned headings, overlaps and bad links. Otherwise deliver editor-ready text and say export/visual verification remains. Version a delivered document; do not claim a device mirror or automatic refresh.
