## Choose the mode

- Investigation: read [investigation.md](references/investigation.md). Start with the record, then seek only evidence reachable through the real permitted route. Copy source identifiers and supporting excerpts verbatim; cite actual page numbers or honest section/time locators.
- Briefing from existing material: read [briefing.md](references/briefing.md). Compile the owner's supplied and designated material; do not fill gaps with unrequested outside research or slip in an unattributed recommendation.
- Review only: read [review.md](references/review.md). Check claims against actual sources and coverage without rewriting.

The source's qualifications and limitations travel with its claims. Never invent URLs, citations, page numbers or quotations; a source not opened is not evidence read. A public-source connector is a capability to inspect, not a promised installation. If none is available, name the real route: the owner opens the source in their browser and uploads the relevant document or excerpt with its address, date and page.
