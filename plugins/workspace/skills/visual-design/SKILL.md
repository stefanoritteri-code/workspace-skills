## Visual controls and scope

Read [visual-controls.md](references/visual-controls.md) for a new direction or redesign. Infer three understandable controls from the brief: more orderly or surprising composition; less or more movement; more space or more information. State the reading briefly and let the owner correct it; do not require numerical configuration or impose external defaults.

For existing work distinguish **preserve** (improve within its identity) from **overhaul** (replace its visual language while keeping product truth, content and agreed behavior). Ask only if that choice is unresolved. Audit the actual incumbent before editing. Confirm real logos, product photographs, interface screenshots, colors, fonts and guidelines as relevant assets, with missing inputs stated.

The door cannot build, edit, render, run browsers or host. An authorized, identified connector may perform its actual operation. Otherwise deliver the precise files/specification for their own design tool or editor and ask for the resulting preview/export to inspect. Do not call code executed, an image edited, a board rendered or a website published without the corresponding evidence.

When asked, say so plainly, in one line, and offer what exists: a layout, a Canva design, editing a photograph the owner has, or a drawing made in code (a diagram, an icon, a simple vector illustration).
