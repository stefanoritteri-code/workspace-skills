# Photograph selection and critique

Work from existing photographs. Establish destination, number, format, tone, accepted references and exclusions. No inherited genre or palette. Confirm what is actually visible and associate a stable filename/number with each frame. A contact sheet needs an explicit number → original mapping; distinguish original, preview and variant. If resolution cannot support focus/noise/skin-detail judgment, state that and request the relevant detail.

For many images request a chosen upload/export or identified permitted connector result, not the entire archive. Creating a contact sheet needs a real compositor/editor route; otherwise deliver a numbered list of images actually seen and call it a list. An upload is not proof of local-only processing.

For each candidate explain eye path, gesture or relation, distraction and suitability. Judge composition, light, moment, final-size readability and reference fit. A technical imperfection can serve the image; do not reject by formula. Give recommended selection, reserve and rejected frames with specific reasons. A sequence needs opening, varied distance/rhythm, no unnecessary doubles and an ending; the best isolated picture may not serve the set.

Offer two to four variants only for a meaningful choice: crop, exposure/balance and chosen treatment. State what changes and what is lost. Monochrome, grain or strong color are options, not defaults. Selection/critique does not authorize generated images, changed faces or invented details.

Without an actual authorized editing connector, provide a recipe for the owner's photo editor: source file, aspect ratio/crop boundaries, requested correction, reference, final format and acceptance check. Keep originals intact and new files for variants. File access does not prove RAW/layer/retouch/export support. Name the editor or local processing step with a test image and success criterion. A recipe is not an edited photograph.

Reopen an actual returned export and compare crop, color, detail and dimensions to original and destination. Without seeing it, verification remains open. Separate executed variants from proposals and retain rejected versions/reasons. No implicit deletion, move or publication.

Deliver stable file IDs, selection/reason/reserve and limits, and variant purpose/change/status. Preserve a contrary owner choice and their reason without rewriting the critique. File via [landing.md](landing.md): Photograph selection page with references, recipes and actual outcomes; exact choice with `page`; dated export/proof check if chosen. The text page does not save originals.
