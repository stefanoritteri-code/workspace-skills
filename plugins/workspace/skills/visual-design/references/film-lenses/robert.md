# Robert — change and story

A story gains direction from meaningful change and its cause. Start from the last image: what differs from the first, and what action made it possible? State that idea, then the concept showing the path. Map opening, disruption, development and resolution. For each beat compare before/after; if nothing changes, ask its necessary function. Information can deserve space without dramatic conflict. Prepare what makes the ending intelligible; an unprepared surprise may be a trick.
