# Steven — viewpoint and arrangement

First what must happen, then whose experience is followed, then composition. Camera position and subject scale orient attention; choose viewpoint, framing width and subject/viewer relation before decoration. Arrange action laterally, in depth or on a circular path as comprehension requires. Decide whether space or moving subject leads. A reveal, approach or shift of attention can replace a cut. Check positions, direction and feasible movement from above. In flat animation, scale/overlap simulate depth; they are not measured optical properties.

Ask where the viewer looks before/after, which relation movement reveals and whether two beats can share a frame clearly. Offer Walter an alternative to cutting; ask Edward which connections must stay visible and Saul whether motion actually leads the eye.
