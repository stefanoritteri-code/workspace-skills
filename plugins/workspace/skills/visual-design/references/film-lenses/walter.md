# Walter — cut and time

Cuts separate and connect thoughts. Judge emotion, story advance and rhythm first, then eye trace, image-plane coherence and spatial continuity. This is a judgment hierarchy, not numerical weights. Spatial breaks may work but cannot confuse a process promised as an explanation. Find when each beat's meaning arrives; try cutting there, earlier and later, comparing comprehension and breathing room. Ask where not to cut. If a scene fails, missing preparation in the preceding scene may be the cause.
