Establish audience, intended understanding/feeling, viewing surface, duration, format, language, sound, constraints and actual materials. Retrieve live decisions and accepted/rejected examples. Use existing answers. New film/format: full treatment and relevant lenses. Accepted-format variant: adapt board/timing/checks, no compulsory council. Simple assembly: give source files/order to an editor without imposing a story. Mixed work identifies the narrative part and who executes inserts.

If the owner wants to participate in choices, take one beat at a time. If they request a complete treatment, deliver it without ritual pauses. Two or three developed directions only for a real taste/method fork. Do not choose an unresolved essential preference on their behalf.

## Treatment

Open with what should stay with the viewer and the real claim the film supports. In fiction distinguish brief facts, invented events and assertions about the world; invented events need no spurious documentary proof.

Deliver concept in one sentence with opening, turn and resolution; proposed/confirmed total duration, aspect, resolution, delivery format, surface, language, audio and frame rate; timed board; sound brief; requested format variants; and production handoff.

Each board row: actual visual reference; what is seen; narrative function; on-screen words; optional voice; movement; start/end/duration; transition; reason to keep watching; source for factual claims. Use nonoverlapping end-exclusive frame ranges: `0–72` then `72–144` each last 72 frames. Check sum against total and agreed frame rate, including spoken/text-reading time. Example arithmetic only: 12 seconds at 24 fps = 288 frames. If timing is only in seconds, label it provisional. No default frame rate.

A visual reference is an image actually seen, supplied sketch or time-labeled frame; description is not an image. With no images deliver a **provisional text board**, identify panels to draw/upload and do not claim production readiness. Sound brief shares picture timing: emotional arc, entrances/silences, sync points, energy and music/effect/voice balance. It is a request to a composer/editor, not composed audio.

Variants address only required surfaces: crop, subtitles or silent-viewing text, real-size readability and editor-checked margins. Do not assume platform standards. For each production element name available/missing input, operation, agreed executor, real editor/build and acceptance evidence.

## Checks in order

**True:** each factual claim, including implied motion, has pertinent dated evidence. A planned feature cannot look currently available. Remove unsupported claims or mark unresolved; an essential unsupported step blocks readiness. Fiction remains identified.

**Clear:** an actual newcomer reads/sees the board and reports what they understood. Preserve the real response. Without that test, state “new-reader check pending”; same-model simulation is self-review.

**Engaging:** each beat has a specific reason for attention/emotion and a moment when meaning arrives. Inspect opening, holds, transitions and duration. A board supports a design judgment; actual fluidity, temporal readability and sustained attention require animatic/edit evidence.

Report each result and proof. High-exposure work may need a real fresh person/conversation to check claims, never falsely reported as done. The door cannot animate, compose, render or export video. Use an identified capable connector or a named human editor/local build with explicit steps for panels, edit, animatic, sound, render and export inspection. A folder connection supplies none by itself.

File via [landing.md](landing.md): Film page with treatment, sources and checks; exact owner choice with `page`; chosen deadline task for animatic or film verification. Keep incomplete states visible, with no invented verdicts or dates.
