# Methods from authors the owner follows

Ask which authors/methods matter only if the owner has not already specified them and invoking a method would help. Do not infer a favorite from a demographic, topic or another owner's record. There is no preinstalled coach personality or private-author dossier.

Read relevant public method through a permitted door-held source, an owner-supplied excerpt/export with provenance, or an identified available public-source connector. Prefer the author's actual public explanation and preserve title, date, page/section and qualifications. Do not reconstruct quotations or claim complete-corpus knowledge from a summary. Only public method may inform the work; never seek living people's private health, family, messages or files. No real person is present or endorsing the plan.

Create a small method card for this use: actual source/locator; principle in neutral language; what the author actually says; what is our adaptation; applicability conditions; evidence limits; conflict with existing plan/constraints; one test relevant to the owner. If the material is unavailable, state that and ask for the relevant excerpt; continue a data-based review without invented attribution.

Do not treat popularity, credentials or a public training anecdote as proof the method suits this owner. Compare the method with their goal, experience, reported response, schedule and clinician restrictions. A non-clinical habit principle may inform scheduling or consistency; a medical claim remains outside this skill's advice scope. Do not give a health prescription or contradict a doctor through an author's authority.

If two methods conflict, explain the concrete tradeoff and what observation could distinguish them. Offer a small reversible trial within established non-clinical bounds, with owner choice and review criterion. Keep source claim, generated proposal, adopted decision and observed result distinct. An author's rule is not automatically the owner's live rule; use [landing.md](landing.md) only after actual adoption in their own words.
