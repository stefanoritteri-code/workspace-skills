# Research brief and creation handoffs

> Reference for writing-pack-topic-research. Read when writing the brief or handing an opportunity to creation.

Topic Research should end in a decision the owner can reuse, not a pile of links. The narrative artifact is the Topic Research Brief. Creation handoffs carry the selected opportunity and its evidence into the next skill without forcing them to repeat the research.

## Topic Research Brief contract

Title: `Topic Research Brief: <topic>`. Target 600 to 900 words; go longer only when the evidence genuinely earns the extra length. Lead with the recommendation:

1. **Recommended direction:** why it wins, what tradeoff it makes, and what evidence could change the decision.
2. **Decision and scope:** the decision being made and its material boundaries, in two lines at most.
3. **Market snapshot:** the strongest conversations, notable counter-signals, and overall confidence.
4. **Ranked opportunities:** three to five opportunity cards with market evidence, gap, fit, risk, and smallest test.
5. **Fit evidence:** only the owner's material actually read and relevant to their credibility or positioning.
6. **Next experiment:** the smallest content piece that can test the thesis.
7. **Source receipts:** canonical links and the role each pivotal source played.

For cluster-level detail, reference the Topic Opportunity Map instead of repeating it. Separate observed source facts from analytical inference. Do not paste large source passages. Omit any section with nothing real to put in it, and never restate their own inputs back to them.

Where it lives: in full in the chat; in the room, on their yes, a page via `write_page` (`author: counterpart`), revisions as dated appends; in the terminal, the brain's notebooks, under `ideas-lab/topic-research/brief-<topic-slug>.md`, revised in place.

## Handoff packet

A creation handoff contains the selected opportunity, intended audience, core tension, evidence-backed promise, distinct point of view, owned proof, representative sources, constraints, and the test being run. It does not contain the entire raw corpus.

Route by outcome:

- `writing-pack-substack-notes` — a portfolio of Substack Notes.
- `writing-pack-substack-newsletter` — a full issue of the owner's newsletter.
- the room's **writing** skill — a single post, LinkedIn, press or campaign copy in their voice.
- the room's **visual-design** skill — a video, film or visual concept.
- `writing-pack-product-design` / `writing-pack-offer-design` — when the opportunity is a product or offer rather than content.

Do not draft automatically just because research is complete. Offer the most relevant next step. Carry the evidence packet forward so the next skill does not repeat broad discovery or lose the research boundary.

## Continuity

Later requests to refresh, narrow, compare, or create retrieve the existing brief and map first. When evidence changes, update the map and brief before handing off a stale recommendation.

A good handoff preserves reasoning while reducing context. The next skill should know what was selected, why it was selected, which sources support it, and what remains uncertain.
